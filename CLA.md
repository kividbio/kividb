## How to sign

**Individuals.** Open a pull request to the Project and add a comment to it that reads:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

By posting that comment from Your account, You agree to Part A of this Agreement for that
Contribution and for all Your future Contributions to the Project. You need to sign only
once.

**Entities.** An authorized signatory of the Entity signs Part B in the same way, by
commenting on a pull request with the sentence above followed by the Entity's legal name
and the GitHub usernames of its Designated Contributors, or contacts the Company through
[kividb.io](https://kividb.io) to sign it in writing. Each Designated Contributor also
signs Part A.

The Company keeps a record of each signature.
